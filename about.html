About Rayvern Chng

A financial advisor who puts your goals, your family, and your legacy at the centre of every decision.

My Story

More Than Numbers — It's About People

I didn't become a financial advisor because I love spreadsheets—though I do appreciate a well-structured plan. I became one because I saw too many business owners and families face devastating financial consequences that could have been prevented with the right guidance at the right time.

As a certified financial advisor based in Singapore, I've dedicated my career to helping business owners, managing directors, HR leaders, and corporate decision-makers build financial strategies that truly protect what matters most. Whether it's ensuring a company survives the sudden loss of its founder, creating an estate plan that keeps a family's wealth intact across generations, or building a lifelong care plan for a child with special needs—these aren't just "services" to me. They're commitments I make to real people facing real challenges.

My qualifications—AWP (CFP), IBFQ Level 1, and AEPP—give me the technical foundation to navigate complex financial landscapes. My recognition as an MDRT member reflects the standard of excellence and ethical practice I hold myself to. But what truly sets my approach apart is this: I listen first. I understand your situation, your concerns, and your aspirations before I ever recommend a solution.

My Philosophy

Client-First, Always

Listen First

Every financial plan begins with understanding. Before I recommend any solution, I take the time to understand your unique situation, your concerns, and the goals that truly matter to you and your family.

Plan with Precision

Vague advice helps no one. I provide structured, actionable strategies built on rigorous analysis. Every recommendation is backed by expertise and tailored to your specific circumstances—not a one-size-fits-all template.

Partner for Life

Financial planning isn't a one-time transaction. I build long-term relationships with my clients, reviewing and refining strategies as your business grows, your family evolves, and your goals shift over time.

Credentials & Recognition

Qualified, Recognised, Trusted

My professional qualifications and industry recognition reflect my commitment to delivering the highest standard of financial advisory services.

CFP

AWP (CFP)

Associate Wealth Planner — Singapore's equivalent of the internationally recognised Certified Financial Planner® designation. Demonstrates comprehensive competency across investment, insurance, tax, retirement, and estate planning.

IBF

IBFQ Level 1

Insurance Business Faculty Qualification — professional certification demonstrating expertise in insurance advisory, risk assessment, and business protection strategies under Singapore's regulatory framework.

AEP

AEPP

Advanced Estate Planning Practitioner — specialist certification in estate planning, including wills, trusts, succession planning, and wealth transfer strategies specific to Singapore law.

MDR

MDRT

Million Dollar Round Table — the premier association of the world's top financial professionals, representing the top percentage globally. MDRT members are recognised for exceptional professional knowledge, ethical conduct, and outstanding client service.
