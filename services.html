Service 01

Business & Corporate Insurance

What would happen to your business if you or a key partner could no longer work tomorrow? For many Singapore businesses, the honest answer is: it would be at serious risk. Business insurance isn't just about compliance—it's about ensuring the enterprise you've built can survive the unexpected and continue to thrive.

As a specialist in corporate insurance advisory, I work with business owners, managing directors, and heads of HR to design comprehensive coverage strategies that protect against the financial impact of losing key personnel, ensure employee welfare, and maintain business continuity.

Key Person Insurance — Protect your business against the financial impact of losing a founder, director, or critical employee. Covers revenue loss, recruitment costs, and debt obligations.
Group Insurance & Employee Benefits — Attract and retain top talent with comprehensive group health, life, and disability coverage tailored to your team's needs and your budget.
Business Continuity Planning — Ensure your operations can continue during disruption. I help structure insurance and financial reserves to keep your business running smoothly.
Buy-Sell Agreement Funding — When a business partner exits, proper funding ensures a smooth transition of ownership without draining business capital.

Service 02

Financial Advisory & Wealth Planning

Building wealth is one challenge. Preserving and growing it while managing the complexities of a business, a family, and Singapore's regulatory landscape is another entirely. Without a clear financial strategy, even successful business owners can find themselves exposed to unnecessary risk, inefficient tax structures, and an uncertain retirement.

I provide personalised financial advisory that goes beyond generic investment advice. Every strategy I create is built around your specific goals, risk tolerance, and timeline—whether you're accumulating wealth in your peak earning years, planning for a comfortable retirement, or structuring your assets for maximum efficiency.

Wealth Accumulation Strategies — Structured investment plans designed to grow your assets systematically, balancing growth with risk management appropriate to your stage of life.
Retirement Planning — Comprehensive retirement projections and income strategies, including CPF optimisation, ensuring you maintain your lifestyle long after you stop working.
Investment Strategy & Review — Regular portfolio reviews and asset allocation advice, helping you stay on track towards your financial goals while adapting to market changes.
Risk Management & Protection — Identify and address gaps in your financial safety net, ensuring your income, health, and critical illness needs are comprehensively covered.

Service 03

Estate Planning & Succession

In Singapore, dying without a will means the Intestate Succession Act determines how your assets are distributed—and it rarely matches what you would have chosen. For business owners, the consequences can be even more severe: family disputes, frozen business accounts, and a legacy that unravels instead of enduring.

As an Advanced Estate Planning Practitioner (AEPP), I guide business owners, high-net-worth individuals, and families through the process of structuring their estates to ensure wealth is preserved, transferred efficiently, and protected from unnecessary disputes and taxes. My approach integrates business succession with personal estate planning for a seamless, comprehensive solution.

Will Writing & Review — Ensure your assets are distributed according to your wishes. I work with legal partners to draft and review wills that reflect your intentions with precision.
Trust Structures — Living trusts, testamentary trusts, and insurance trusts to protect assets, manage distribution timing, and provide for beneficiaries exactly as you intend.
Business Succession Planning — Ensure your company transitions smoothly to the next generation or a chosen successor, preserving value and avoiding operational disruption.
Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) — Appoint trusted individuals to make decisions on your behalf should you become mentally incapacitated. Essential for every business owner.

Service 04

Special Needs Advisory

For parents and caregivers of loved ones with special needs, the question that weighs heaviest is often the simplest: "Who will take care of them when I'm gone?" It's a deeply personal concern, and it deserves a deeply personal solution—not a generic financial plan.

I specialise in creating comprehensive financial plans for families with special needs dependants in Singapore. This means understanding the unique government schemes available, the role of trusts and insurance in providing lifelong security, and the emotional landscape that families navigate every day. My goal is to give you peace of mind—the certainty that your loved one will be cared for, with dignity and security, no matter what the future holds.

SNTC Trust Planning — The Special Needs Trust Company (SNTC) is Singapore's only non-profit trust company dedicated to persons with special needs. I help families set up and fund SNTC trusts to provide long-term financial security.
CPF Special Needs Savings Scheme (SNSS) — Nominate your CPF savings to provide regular monthly payouts to your special needs dependant, rather than a lump sum. I guide families through the application and optimisation process.
Insurance & Care Funding — Structure insurance policies to fund long-term care, therapy, and living expenses for your special needs loved one, with proceeds channelled through appropriate trust structures.
Letter of Intent & Care Plan — Document your loved one's routines, preferences, medical needs, and care instructions to ensure continuity of care regardless of changes in caregivers.
