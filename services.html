Service 01

Business & Corporate Insurance

In Singapore, SMEs make up 99% of all enterprises — yet studies show that over 60% lack adequate key person coverage. What would happen to your business if you or a key partner could no longer work tomorrow? For most, the honest answer is: revenue drops, credit lines freeze, and clients walk. Business insurance isn't about compliance — it's about ensuring the enterprise you've built can survive the unexpected.

I work with business owners, managing directors, and HR leaders across industries — from tech startups to established F&B groups — to design corporate insurance strategies that protect against personnel loss, ensure employee welfare, and maintain operational continuity. Every solution is benchmarked against Singapore's regulatory requirements and your industry's specific risk profile.

Key Person Insurance — Protect your business against the financial impact of losing a founder, director, or critical employee. Covers revenue loss, recruitment costs, and debt obligations.
Group Insurance & Employee Benefits — Attract and retain top talent with comprehensive group health, life, and disability coverage tailored to your team's needs and your budget.
Business Continuity Planning — Ensure your operations can continue during disruption. I help structure insurance and financial reserves to keep your business running smoothly.
Buy-Sell Agreement Funding — When a business partner exits, proper funding ensures a smooth transition of ownership without draining business capital.

Service 02

Financial Advisory & Wealth Planning

Singapore's median household income crossed S$10,000 per month in 2023 — yet many high-earning business owners have no structured plan for the wealth they generate. Building wealth is one challenge. Preserving and growing it while managing business cashflow, family obligations, CPF optimisation, and Singapore's evolving regulatory landscape is another entirely.

I provide personalised financial advisory that goes beyond generic investment advice. Every strategy is built around your specific goals, risk tolerance, and timeline — integrating your business financials with your personal wealth to create a unified plan. Whether you're in your peak earning years, planning for retirement, or restructuring after a business exit, I help you move from reactive to strategic.

Wealth Accumulation Strategies — Structured investment plans using diversified portfolios, SRS accounts, and tax-efficient vehicles to grow your assets systematically while managing downside risk.
Retirement Planning — Comprehensive retirement projections and income strategies, including CPF optimisation, ensuring you maintain your lifestyle long after you stop working.
Investment Strategy & Review — Regular portfolio reviews and asset allocation advice, helping you stay on track towards your financial goals while adapting to market changes.
Risk Management & Protection — Identify and address gaps in your financial safety net, ensuring your income, health, and critical illness needs are comprehensively covered.

Service 03

Estate Planning & Succession

More than 60% of Singaporeans still don't have a will. Under the Intestate Succession Act, your assets are distributed by a fixed formula — one that rarely reflects your actual wishes. For business owners, the consequences can be devastating: probate delays freeze company accounts, shareholder disputes paralyse operations, and a legacy you spent decades building can unravel in months.

As an Advanced Estate Planning Practitioner (AEPP), I guide business owners, HNW individuals, and multi-generational families through structuring their estates for efficient wealth transfer, dispute prevention, and long-term preservation. My approach integrates business succession with personal estate planning — covering wills, trusts, LPA, CPF nominations, and insurance nominations as one coordinated strategy.

Will Writing & Review — Ensure your assets are distributed according to your wishes. I work with legal partners to draft and review wills that reflect your intentions with precision.
Trust Structures — Living trusts, testamentary trusts, and insurance trusts to protect assets, manage distribution timing, and provide for beneficiaries exactly as you intend.
Business Succession Planning — Ensure your company transitions smoothly to the next generation or a chosen successor, preserving value and avoiding operational disruption.
Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) — Appoint trusted individuals to make decisions on your behalf should you become mentally incapacitated. Essential for every business owner.

Service 04

Special Needs Advisory

For parents and caregivers of loved ones with special needs, one question weighs heaviest: "Who will take care of them when I'm gone?" In Singapore, approximately 3–5% of the population lives with some form of disability — yet many families lack a structured financial plan for lifelong care. A generic financial plan won't work here. This requires specialised knowledge and deep empathy.

I create comprehensive financial plans for families with special needs dependants, drawing on Singapore's dedicated support infrastructure — SNTC trusts, CPF SNSS, Enabling Masterplan initiatives, and specialised insurance structures. I work closely with families to understand not just the financial picture, but the daily realities of caregiving, the support networks in place, and the long-term care vision. My goal is to give you certainty — that your loved one will be cared for with dignity and security, no matter what the future holds.

SNTC Trust Planning — The Special Needs Trust Company (SNTC) is Singapore's only non-profit trust company dedicated to persons with special needs. I help families set up and fund SNTC trusts to provide long-term financial security.
CPF Special Needs Savings Scheme (SNSS) — Nominate your CPF savings to provide regular monthly payouts to your special needs dependant, rather than a lump sum. I guide families through the application and optimisation process.
Insurance & Care Funding — Structure insurance policies to fund long-term care, therapy, and living expenses for your special needs loved one, with proceeds channelled through appropriate trust structures.
Letter of Intent & Care Plan — Document your loved one's routines, preferences, medical needs, and care instructions to ensure continuity of care regardless of changes in caregivers.
