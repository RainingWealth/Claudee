# Rayvern Chng — Business Profile

## Who We Are
Rayvern Chng is a certified financial advisor based in Singapore, specialising in business insurance, corporate financial planning, estate planning, and special needs advisory.

## Credentials
- AWP (CFP) — Associate Wealth Planner / Certified Financial Planner
- IBFQ Level 1 — Insurance Business Faculty Qualification
- AEPP — Advanced Estate Planning Practitioner
- MDRT — Million Dollar Round Table qualifier

## Services

### 1. Business & Corporate Insurance
- Key person insurance — protect against losing founders, directors, critical staff
- Group insurance & employee benefits — attract and retain talent
- Business continuity planning — keep operations running through disruption
- Buy-sell agreement funding — smooth ownership transitions

### 2. Financial Advisory & Wealth Planning
- Wealth accumulation strategies
- Retirement planning (including CPF optimisation)
- Investment strategy & portfolio review
- Risk management & protection planning

### 3. Estate Planning & Succession
- Will writing & review
- Trust structures (living trusts, testamentary trusts, insurance trusts)
- Business succession planning
- Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA)

### 4. Special Needs Advisory
- SNTC (Special Needs Trust Company) trust planning
- CPF Special Needs Savings Scheme (SNSS) nominations
- Insurance & care funding through trusts
- Letter of Intent & care plan documentation

## Target Audience
- Business owners / founders (primary)

- Corporate leaders and decision-makers
