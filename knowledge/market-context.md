# Singapore Financial Advisory Market

## Market Context
- Singapore is a major financial hub with sophisticated regulatory framework (MAS)
- High density of HNW individuals and family offices
- CPF (Central Provident Fund) is unique to Singapore — key part of retirement/estate planning
- Strong demand for business insurance among SMEs (over 99% of enterprises in Singapore)
- Growing awareness of special needs financial planning

## Common Industry Terms
- CPF — Central Provident Fund (mandatory savings scheme)
- SRS — Supplementary Retirement Scheme
- SNTC — Special Needs Trust Company (Singapore's only non-profit trust for special needs)
- SNSS — CPF Special Needs Savings Scheme
- LPA — Lasting Power of Attorney
- ISA — Intestate Succession Act (applies when there's no will)
- MAS — Monetary Authority of Singapore (regulator)
- MDRT — Million Dollar Round Table (top financial advisor recognition)
- ILP — Investment-Linked Policy
- GE — Group Employee benefits

## Target Client Segments

### Business Owners / SME Founders
- Pain: "What if something happens to me or my partner?"
- Trigger: New business partner, major contract, expansion
- Hot button: Business survival, legacy, family security
- DISC tendency: Often D or I types

### HR Heads / Corporate Leaders
- Pain: "We're losing talent to competitors with better benefits"
- Trigger: Employee turnover, benefits review cycle, company growth
- Hot button: Talent retention, cost efficiency, compliance
- DISC tendency: Often S or C types

### HNW Individuals & Families
- Pain: "My wealth is growing but I'm not sure it's protected"
- Trigger: Major liquidity event, new child, ageing parents
- Hot button: Wealth preservation, tax efficiency, legacy
- DISC tendency: Varies — expect sophisticated, well-informed buyers

### Special Needs Families
- Pain: "Who takes care of my child when I'm gone?"
- Trigger: Diagnosis, transition milestones, parent ageing
- Hot button: Peace of mind, long-term security, quality of care
- DISC tendency: Often S types — need patience, reassurance, empathy

## Competitor Landscape
- Large agencies (AIA, Prudential, Great Eastern) — brand trust but less personal
- Independent financial advisors — similar positioning, differentiation through niche expertise
- Online robo-advisors — low-cost but no human touch for complex needs
- Rayvern's edge: niche depth (business + estate + special needs), personal approach, AEPP/MDRT credibility
