# Angle 3 — Lead Form: Mid-career Financial Confidence Gap

**Form Type:** Facebook Instant Form — "Higher Intent" setting
**Critical Rule:** This angle attracts the broadest audience. The form MUST do the heavy qualification work. Do NOT invite all leads to 1:1 meetings — your calendar will fill with unqualified leads.

**Hard Qualification Threshold for Meeting:**
- Monthly household income: S$8,000+ AND
- Investable assets (outside CPF/HDB): S$10,000+

Anyone below these thresholds goes to email nurture + group webinar, NOT a 1:1 meeting.

## Questions

### Auto-fill Fields
1. **Full Name** *(required)*
2. **Phone Number** *(required — WhatsApp number)*
3. **Email Address** *(optional, but recommended for nurture sequence)*

### Custom Questions

**Question 1:**
> How old are you?

Answer options:
- 28–33
- 34–39
- 40–45
- 46–50
- 51+

*Qualification note:*
- 34–45 = Prime target (income + runway)
- 28–33 = Good for relationship building, lower immediate investment capacity
- 46–50 = Good, but pivot conversation toward retirement urgency
- 51+ = Route to Angle 2 (CPF55) audience in CRM; send both guides

---

**Question 2:**
> What's your approximate monthly household income? (Include spouse/partner if applicable)

Answer options:
- Below S$4,000
- S$4,000–S$8,000
- S$8,000–S$15,000
- S$15,000–S$25,000
- Above S$25,000

*Qualification note:*
- Below S$4k = Nurture only. Different product fit. No meeting push.
- S$4k–8k = Standard follow-up. Position framing around starting small.
- S$8k+ = Priority lead. Meeting invitation after soft qualifier on call.
- S$15k+ = High-value. Wealth management conversation potential. Fast-track.

---

**Question 3:**
> How would you describe where you are financially right now?

Answer options:
- Paycheck-to-paycheck — spending matches (or exceeds) income
- Saving, but not sure if it's enough or in the right places
- Have investments, but no clear retirement plan
- Fairly organised but haven't reviewed in a while
- Just starting to think about this

*Qualification note:*
- "Saving but unsure" and "have investments but no plan" = strongest meeting signals
- "Paycheck-to-paycheck" = high empathy, but check income first — may need different conversation
- "Just starting" = nurture track unless age 38+ with decent income
- "Fairly organised but haven't reviewed" = still valuable — frame meeting as efficiency audit

---

**Question 4:**
> Do you have savings or investments outside of CPF and your HDB?

Answer options:
- Less than S$10,000
- S$10,000–S$50,000
- S$50,000–S$200,000
- Above S$200,000
- Not much / all locked in CPF and HDB

*This is your hardest qualifier.*

*Qualification note:*
- Less than S$10k = Nurture track. Offer "start your investment journey" group session, not 1:1.
- S$10k–50k = Standard lead. Meeting possible if income qualifies.
- S$50k+ = Priority. Clear investable assets to work with.
- S$200k+ = High-value. Fast-track to meeting.
- "All in CPF/HDB" = Important case — may not have investable assets for ILP/UT but could benefit from SRS strategy. Different conversation.

---

**Question 5 (Optional — show if CPL allows):**
> Are you currently working with a financial adviser?

Answer options:
- No — managing everything myself
- Had one before, no longer in contact
- Have one, but haven't reviewed my plan in 2+ years
- Yes — regular reviews with an active adviser

*Qualification note: Use to frame conversation. "No" = open opportunity. "Haven't reviewed" = "your plan is probably pre-2023 — a lot has changed."*

## Qualification Decision Tree

```
Lead comes in
      │
      ├── Income S$8k+ AND investable assets S$10k+?
      │         → PRIORITY MEETING TRACK
      │           WhatsApp within 5 min
      │           Offer: "30-min Freedom Number session"
      │
      ├── Income S$4–8k AND assets $10–50k?
      │         → STANDARD TRACK
      │           WhatsApp within 2 hours
      │           Offer: "Let's calculate your number together"
      │           Book only if call reveals clear gap + willingness
      │
      ├── Income S$4–8k AND assets under $10k?
      │         → NURTURE TRACK
      │           No direct meeting push
      │           Send to email sequence
      │           Invite to free group webinar when available
      │
      ├── Income below S$4k?
      │         → LOW PRIORITY
      │           Send guide, no follow-up call
      │           Add to long-term nurture email list
      │
      └── Age 51+ answer?
                → Flag in CRM: "Consider CPF55 angle"
                   Send BOTH Freedom Number Audit AND CPF55 Readiness Report
                   Pivot follow-up to CPF/retirement urgency
```

## Group Webinar Offer (for non-meeting track leads)

For leads who qualify on interest but not on assets/income:

Offer a free "Freedom Number Workshop" webinar (monthly, 45–60 min):
- Walkthrough of the audit live
- Q&A on Singapore-specific questions (CPF, SRS, HDB equity)
- At the end: invite for 1:1 follow-up for anyone who wants personalised analysis

This converts low-ticket leads over time without clogging your calendar.

## Technical Setup Notes

- Integrate with CRM via Zapier (Meta Leads → Google Sheets → CRM tagging)
- Tag leads by: age band, income tier, assets tier
- Priority leads (income S$8k+ + assets $10k+): Trigger phone notification to you immediately
- Non-priority leads: Auto-send PDF via email, add to 14-day WhatsApp nurture sequence
- Age 51+ leads: Also add to Angle 2 (CPF55) retargeting audience in Meta
