# Angle 3 — Mid-career Financial Confidence Gap: Ad Variants

**Campaign Goal:** Generate high-volume leads from Singapore professionals aged 28–42. Strong insecurity hook drives clicks. Heavy form qualification filters for investment-ready leads. Do NOT send all leads to 1:1 meetings — use webinar/group sessions for unqualified leads.

**Targeting:** Singapore, Age 28–42, interests: personal finance, FIRE, passive income, career, Seedly, The Woke Salaryman, Endowus, Syfe. Exclude: crypto/trading-heavy interests.

**Key rule:** Meeting invitation only for leads with household income S$8k+/month AND investable savings S$10k+. All others go to email/WhatsApp nurture.
