# Angle 3 — Follow-Up Scripts: Mid-career Financial Confidence Gap

**Tone:** Smart, peer-level, direct, slightly energising. This audience is digitally savvy and has seen too many financial ads. Sound like a smart friend who happens to be a financial expert — not a salesperson.

**Critical Rule:** Do NOT push a meeting on every lead. You MUST qualify first. Leads who don't meet the threshold (income S$8k+, investable assets S$10k+) go to nurture, not to your calendar.

**Response Time Target:**
- Priority leads: Within 5 minutes
- Standard leads: Within 2 hours
- Nurture track: Email auto-sequence; WhatsApp within 24 hours

## Step 2 — Soft Qualification (When they reply)

Before booking any meeting, ask (conversationally, not all at once):

> "Quick question — roughly what does your monthly household income look like? Just a ballpark is fine."

> "And do you have any investments or savings outside of CPF and HDB? Even if it's a relatively small amount?"

> "What's your rough target retirement age — or is it more of an 'as early as possible' feeling?"

**Internal decision after their answers:**
- Income S$8k+ AND assets S$10k+ → Invite to meeting
- Income S$4–8k AND assets S$10k+ → Conditional invite, frame meeting as exploration
- Below both thresholds → Offer webinar or send nurture sequence

## Objection Handling

### "I'm not sure I can afford to invest right now"
> "That's worth exploring properly. Sometimes the issue is genuinely cash flow — and sometimes it's that money is going to the wrong places without a clear plan. Even if the answer is 'wait 6 months', it's worth knowing what you're waiting for.
>
> Would a 20-minute call just to map it out be useful? No obligation to do anything — just clarity."

### "I've heard too many pitches from financial advisers"
> "I get that — the industry has a credibility problem. What I can offer is this: the first session is 100% diagnostic. I'll show you your numbers and explain your options. Whether you want to take it further is entirely your call. If you decide you don't want to do anything, I'll respect that.
>
> Would a 'no recommendation' first call feel more comfortable to you?"

### "I'll do it myself — I'm quite into personal finance"
> "That's great — honestly the people who've read about FIRE and SG-focused investing are my favourite clients. They ask better questions. My value add is usually in the CPF/SRS optimisation, tax angles, and making sure the overall portfolio is coherent rather than a collection of random decisions.
>
> Would a 20-minute sanity check be useful — just to make sure what you have built is consistent?"

### "I need to talk to my spouse first"
> "Absolutely — and these conversations are always better when both of you are in it. Would it be possible to find a time when your spouse could join the call? Even just for the first half?"

### "I'm worried this is just about selling me insurance or ILPs"
> "Legitimate concern. I'll be honest: if insurance ends up being part of the picture, I'll explain why — and if it doesn't make sense, I'll tell you that too. The first session has zero product recommendations. It's just your numbers. Does that work for you?"
