# Meta Campaign Strategy — Singapore Financial Advisory
## Master Reference Document

---

## Goal Statement

**Primary Goal:** Generate high-volume investment planning leads on Facebook/Instagram at sustainable CPL, converting into booked meetings with meaningful investment potential.

**Core KPI Stack:**
1. Low CPL (target: $2–$15 depending on angle)
2. High meeting-book rate (target: 10–25% of qualified leads)
3. High meeting show-up rate (target: 60%+)
4. Leads with actionable investment capacity (S$10k+ investable minimum)

---

## Campaign Ladder

| Campaign | Angle | CPL Target | Volume | Meeting Quality | Priority |
|----------|-------|-----------|--------|-----------------|----------|
| Angle 1 | Maternity → Education Gap | S$2–5 | Very High | Medium-High | Primary (run first) |
| Angle 2 | CPF 55 Rule Change | S$20–40 | Medium | Very High | Secondary |
| Angle 3 | Mid-career Confidence Gap | S$10–25 | High | Medium (filtered) | Top-up |

**Budget Allocation Recommendation:**
- 60% → Angle 1 (Maternity/Education)
- 25% → Angle 2 (CPF 55)
- 15% → Angle 3 (Mid-career)

Start with Angle 1 only for the first 2 weeks to establish baseline CPL. Add Angle 2 in week 3. Add Angle 3 in week 5 after retargeting audience is large enough.

## Audience Targeting Specs (Meta Ads Manager)

### Angle 1 — Maternity/Education (Primary)
**Location:** Singapore
**Age:** 24–40
**Gender:** All (skew female for maternity; both for education follow-up)
**Interests (combine with OR):**
- Pregnancy, Parenting, Babies & Toddlers
- NUS / NTU / SMU (university interest)
- Baby products (Mothercare, Enfamil, Pampers)
- Financial planning, Insurance
**Behaviours:** New parents (Facebook parenting behaviour)
**Exclude:** Anyone who has already submitted a lead form

### Angle 2 — CPF 55 (Secondary)
**Location:** Singapore
**Age:** 50–58
**Gender:** All
**Interests:**
- CPF, Central Provident Fund
- Retirement planning, Retirement savings
- Singapore government schemes
**Income signals:** Target "Top 25% household income" if available
**Lookalike:** 1–3% lookalike from warm Angle 1 audience who engaged/clicked

### Angle 3 — Mid-career (Top-up)
**Location:** Singapore
**Age:** 28–42
**Gender:** All
**Interests:**
- Financial independence, FIRE movement
- Passive income, Side hustle
- Investment (stocks, REITs, unit trusts)
- Career burnout, Work-life balance
- Seedly, The Woke Salaryman, Endowus, Syfe
**Exclude:** Interests too heavy in crypto/trading (lowers quality)

## Retargeting Logic

**Retarget with Angle 2 ads:**
- Everyone who downloaded Angle 1 lead magnet but did NOT book a meeting (30-day window)
- Add messaging: "Now that baby is home, are YOU on track for retirement?"

**Retarget with Angle 3 ads:**
- Website visitors and video viewers from Angle 1 campaign
- Lookalike of Angle 1 converters aged 28–40

**Retarget with Angle 2 (CPF):**
- Engage Angle 1 leads whose children are older (run "education + retirement" dual angle)

---

## Creative Principles (All Angles)

1. **Lead with data, not claims.** "52% of parents will borrow for uni" > "Plan for your child's future."
2. **Make the gap concrete.** Use specific dollar amounts: "FRS payout ~$1,500/month vs your goal of $3,000."
3. **The offer is a deliverable, not a meeting.** "Get your personalised projection" > "Book a consultation."
4. **Meet compliance.** No guaranteed return claims. Use "may", "projected", "based on current rates." No specific investment products named.
5. **Mobile-first.** 80%+ of Facebook traffic is mobile. Use large text, clean visuals, max 2 stat callouts per image.

---

## Compliance Reminders (Singapore MAS)

- All ads must carry an advisory disclaimer if making performance/return references
- No absolute return guarantees ("you WILL earn", "guaranteed income")
- Use conditional language: "CPF LIFE MAY pay approximately...", "projections based on current fee rates"
- For educational lead magnets: include "This is for informational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice"
- If running as a tied representative, ensure company approvals as required
