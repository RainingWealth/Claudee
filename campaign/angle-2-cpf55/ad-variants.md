# Angle 2 — CPF 55 Rule Change: Ad Variants

**Campaign Goal:** Generate high-intent leads from CPF holders aged 50–58. Lower volume than Angle 1 but significantly higher meeting conversion and ticket size. Framed around policy change urgency + concrete income gap.

**Targeting:** Singapore, Age 50–58, interests: CPF / retirement planning / government schemes. Budget: 25% of total campaign budget.

**Compliance note:** Always use "approximately", "may", "projected", "based on current CPF data" — never guarantee payouts.

---

## Ad Variant A — CPF Gap Awareness (Recommended First Test)
**Emotional Hook:** Security fear + Concrete gap + Time-bound decision

**Primary Text:**
> CPF LIFE at Full Retirement Sum pays approximately S$1,500–1,600 per month.
>
> Most Singaporeans say they need S$2,000–3,500/month to live comfortably in retirement.
>
> That gap — up to S$2,000 every single month — needs to come from somewhere else.
>
> And from 2025, CPF rules have changed: the Special Account closes at 55, the salary ceiling is rising, and contribution rates for workers above 55 are being updated.
>
> Get your free CPF 55 Readiness Report — see exactly what your CPF LIFE may pay, and whether your current plan covers the gap.

**Headline:** Your CPF Gap Could Be $2,000/Month

**Description:** Free personalised CPF 55 readiness report. Takes 2 minutes.

**CTA Button:** Get Free Report

**Creative Direction:** Clean, authoritative. Simple graphic showing two bars: "CPF LIFE FRS payout ~$1,600/month" (shorter bar) vs "Average desired retirement income ~$3,000/month" (taller bar). Clean navy and gold colour scheme. No stock photos — data visual only.

>
> If you have S$200,000+ in CPF and are turning 55 in the next 3 years, this matters.
>
> Download the free CPF 55 Rule Change Explainer — plain English, no jargon, 1 page.

**Headline:** CPF Special Account Closed at 55 — What Now?

**Description:** Free plain-English guide to what the 2025 CPF changes mean for you.

**CTA Button:** Get the Explainer

**Creative Direction:** News-style headline format. Bold black text on white background with a thin red/orange accent bar on left edge (editorial feel). Text graphic: "CPF Rule Change: What 50–58 Year Olds Need to Know in 2025–2026". Feels authoritative, not salesy.

## Ad Variant C — Payout Reality Check
**Emotional Hook:** Social comparison + Fear of not knowing + Calm authority

**Primary Text:**
> Do you know which CPF LIFE tier you're on?
>
> Basic Retirement Sum (BRS): ~S$780–820/month
> Full Retirement Sum (FRS): ~S$1,500–1,600/month
> Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS): ~S$3,180–3,410/month
>
> 30% of active CPF members turning 55 in 2022 didn't even meet FRS.
>
> Most people don't know which tier they're on — or what the gap means for their lifestyle.
>
> In 15 minutes, I can tell you exactly where you stand, what CPF LIFE may pay you, and whether you have a gap that needs planning. Free. No obligation.

**Headline:** BRS, FRS or ERS — Which Are You On?

**Description:** Know your CPF tier and whether you have a retirement income gap. Free 15-min session.

**CTA Button:** Check My CPF

**Creative Direction:** Three-tier visual. A simple pyramid or three-step graphic showing BRS → FRS → ERS with payout bands on the right. Clean white background, navy and gold tones. This is a knowledge-first visual — positions you as the expert before any conversation happens.

## Ad Variant D — Longevity + Lifestyle Gap
**Emotional Hook:** Outliving savings fear + Lifestyle protection + Calm but urgent

**Primary Text:**
> CPF LIFE is designed to pay you for life. But "for life" is only part of the story.
>
> For a typical middle-income retiree in Singapore, CPF LIFE covers an estimated 20–50% of total spending needs.
>
> The other 50–80% must come from your personal savings, investments, or other income sources.
>
> With Singapore's cost of living ranked top globally for most of the past decade, that gap is growing — not shrinking.
>
> If you're 50–58 and want to see your retirement income picture clearly before you turn 65, download the free CPF 55 Retirement Income Gap Report.

**Headline:** CPF LIFE Covers Only 20–50% of Retirement Costs

**Description:** See your full retirement income picture before it's too late. Free report.

**CTA Button:** Get Free Report

**Creative Direction:** Donut chart showing CPF LIFE slice (~30%) vs "Gap to fill" slice (~70%). Large visual, clean. Caption: "CPF LIFE (FRS): ~S$1,600/month. Average retiree needs: ~S$3,000/month. Where does the rest come from?" Source: StashAway 2026 analysis.

| Variant | Hook Type | Predicted CPL | Best For |
|---------|-----------|------------|----------|
| A (CPF Gap) | Gap + urgency | S$20–35 | Broad awareness |
| B (SA Closure) | Policy confusion | S$15–30 | News-jacking urgency |

Run Variant B first — it has the strongest news-hook relevance and SA closure is a real and recent event. Run Variant A as a second test. Then compare CPL and meeting conversion rate.
