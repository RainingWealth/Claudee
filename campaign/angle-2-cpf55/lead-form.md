# Angle 2 — Lead Form: CPF 55 Rule Change

**Form Type:** Facebook Instant Form — "Higher Intent" (review step before submit)
**Form Name:** CPF 55 Readiness Report — Singapore 2025–2026

**This audience is more deliberate.** They will not fill out a form impulsively. The intro card must clearly signal authority and relevance. Keep questions concise and reassuring.

---

**Headline:** "Get Your Free CPF 55 Readiness Report"

>

>

**Image:** Same as ad creative (BRS/FRS/ERS bar chart or policy explainer graphic)

---

## Questions

### Auto-fill Fields
1. **Full Name** *(required)*
2. **Phone Number** *(required — WhatsApp number)*

### Custom Questions

**Question 1:**
> How old are you currently?

Answer options:
- 50–52
- 53–55
- 56–58
- 59–61
- Other (I'm asking for a family member)

*Qualification note: 53–58 = highest urgency (turning 55 soon or just turned 55). 50–52 = still valuable, longer planning window. "For family member" = treat as 50–52 equivalence, confirm on WhatsApp.*

---

**Question 2:**
> Do you know approximately how much you have in your CPF accounts combined?

Answer options:
- Below S$100,000
- S$100,000–S$200,000
- S$200,000–S$400,000
- S$400,000–S$600,000
- Above S$600,000
- Not sure / haven't checked recently

*Qualification note: S$200k+ = strong candidate for meeting. S$400k+ = priority outreach. Below $100k = still send report, but meeting pitch focuses on what can still be done. "Not sure" = treat as mid-range until clarified.*

---

**Question 3:**
> What is your main concern about CPF and retirement right now?

Answer options:
- Understanding what the 2025 changes mean for me
- Whether my CPF LIFE will be enough to live on
- What I can do to increase my CPF payouts before I retire
- When and how much I can withdraw
- I'm not sure — just want to understand better

*Qualification note: "Whether CPF LIFE will be enough" and "increase payouts" = meeting-ready signals. All answers receive the report. Tailor WhatsApp follow-up based on their concern.*

---

**Question 4:**
> Do you have significant savings or investments OUTSIDE of CPF?

Answer options:
- Yes — S$100,000 or more
- Yes — between S$50,000–S$100,000
- Some, but mainly relying on CPF
- No / not much outside CPF

*Qualification note: S$100k+ outside CPF = strong ticket size signal. "Mainly relying on CPF" = urgent gap — frame meeting as critical planning session. "No / not much" = nurture track, offer low-cost planning options.*

## Qualification Decision Tree

```
Lead comes in
      │
      ├── Age 53–58 AND CPF $200k+ AND some savings outside CPF?
      │         → PRIORITY: WhatsApp within 5 min
      │           Offer: "15-min CPF gap projection call"
      │
      ├── Age 50–58 AND CPF $100–200k?
      │         → STANDARD: WhatsApp within 2 hours
      │           Offer: "Walk through your CPF picture — what can still be done"
      │
      ├── Below $100k CPF OR age 50–52 AND main concern is "just want to understand"?
      │         → NURTURE: Send follow-up guide on CPF basics
      │           No hard meeting push — let them initiate
      │
      └── "For a family member" answer:
                → Ask on WhatsApp if they want to attend together
                   Treat as priority if member is 53–58
```
