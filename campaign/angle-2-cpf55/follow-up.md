# Angle 2 — Follow-Up Scripts: CPF 55 Rule Change

**Tone:** Professional, calm, authoritative. Peer-to-peer but expert-led. This audience has dealt with sales pitches before and will exit immediately if they feel pressure. They value clarity and credentials.

**Response Time Target:**
- Priority leads (age 53–58, CPF $200k+): Within 5 minutes
- Standard leads: Within 2 hours
- Nurture leads: Within 24 hours

---

## Step 2 — Soft Qualification (When they reply)

If they engage, ask these in conversational flow (not all at once):

> "Are you currently still working, or are you approaching retirement?"

> "Do you have a rough sense of your CPF balance across your accounts? Just an approximate figure is fine."

> "And when you think about retirement income — if you stopped working tomorrow, what monthly amount would you need to live comfortably?"

> "Do you have investments or savings outside of CPF that you're planning to draw on?"

These 4 questions give you everything you need to decide:
- Whether they're worth booking a meeting
- What meeting framing to use
- What gap size you're dealing with

## Objection Handling

### "I'm fine — CPF should be enough for me"
> "That's a good position to be in. Out of curiosity, do you know roughly what your CPF LIFE monthly payout might be at 65? There's quite a range between BRS, FRS and ERS — just wanting to make sure 'enough' is based on the actual number and not a general assumption."
>
> [If they don't know] "That's exactly what I can show you in 15 minutes — it's worth confirming before you assume."

### "My son/daughter handles my finances"
> "That's great — family involvement in retirement planning is always a good sign. Would it be possible to include them in the call? A 20-minute family session often gives everyone clarity on the plan going forward."

### "I don't trust financial advisers"
> "I completely understand that concern — the industry has a history of product-first advice. My approach is different: I don't recommend anything in the first session. It's purely a review and projection. If at the end you decide you don't need anything, I'll tell you that too. Would a 'no recommendation' first call feel more comfortable?"

### "What are you selling?"
> "Honestly, nothing in the first session. I earn commissions from financial products if a client eventually puts something in place — but that only happens if it makes sense for you. The CPF review itself is free, and there's no obligation afterward."

### "I can't do Zoom — I'm not good with technology"
> "No problem at all — we can do a phone call instead, or meet in person if you're based near [your area]. Which would be easier for you?"
