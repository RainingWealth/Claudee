# Angle 2 — Image Guide: CPF 55 Rule Change

**Design Principle:** This audience (50–58) is discerning and skeptical of flashy ads. Creatives must feel authoritative, informational, and trustworthy — like a professional briefing, not a hard sell. Data-heavy graphics outperform lifestyle photos for this segment.

**Platform specs:**
- Feed: 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
- Stories: 1080x1920px
- Link ad: 1200x628px

---

## Creative Concept 1 — Variant A (SA Closure Explainer)
**Emotional Direction:** Policy authority + Urgency + Clarity

**Image Description:**
Clean editorial graphic. Dark navy background. Bold headline in large white type. Feels like a financial briefing or regulatory notice — familiar authority for this audience.

**Layout:**
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Red accent bar on left edge]          │
│                                          │
│  CPF UPDATE: 2025                        │
│                                          │
│  SPECIAL ACCOUNT                         │
│  CLOSES AT 55                            │
│                                          │
│  What does this mean for your           │
│  retirement income?                      │
│                                          │
│  Free CPF 55 Readiness Report ────→     │
│                                          │
│  [Your Name] | MAS-Licensed | [Phone]   │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Colour Palette:**
- Background: Deep navy (#003366)
- Primary text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Accent bar: Singapore red (#EF233C) — 4px left border only
- CTA line: Gold (#F4A847)
- Your details: Light grey (#CCCCCC)

**Typography:**
- "CPF UPDATE: 2025" — small caps, letter-spaced, white, 16pt
- "SPECIAL ACCOUNT / CLOSES AT 55" — Bebas Neue or Impact, 60pt+, white
- Body — Inter Regular, 18pt, white

**Canva Build Instructions:**
1. Dark navy rectangle fills entire canvas
2. Thin red rectangle left edge (4px wide, full height)
3. Text elements as above
4. Small CPF-adjacent icon (not official CPF logo — use a generic coin/savings icon)

**AI Image Prompt (optional background element):**
> "Abstract Singapore skyline silhouette, very subtle, dark navy monochrome, used as background texture behind text, not visible in foreground, barely perceptible. Professional. --ar 1:1"

**Emotional Direction:** Data shock + Concrete gap + Urgency to act

**Image Description:**
Two-bar comparison chart. Simple. Clean. The visual makes the gap undeniable.

```
│                                          │

```

**Colour Palette:**
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Short bar (CPF LIFE): Steel grey (#6B7280)

**Canva Build Instructions:**

4. Red horizontal bracket between the tops of bars with "S$1,900/month gap" label
5. Source citation in small grey text at bottom

**Note:** This visual is best as the 1200x628px horizontal format for link ads. Also works in 1080x1080 with slight layout adjustment.

## Creative Concept 3 — Variant C (BRS/FRS/ERS Tier Graphic)
**Emotional Direction:** Knowledge + Social comparison + "Which tier am I on?"

**Image Description:**
Three-tier pyramid or three steps. Each tier shows the retirement sum amount and corresponding payout. Creates natural curiosity: "Which one am I on?"

**Layout:**
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Your CPF LIFE payout depends on       │
│  which tier you're in:                  │
│                                          │
│  ERS S$440,800 → ~S$3,400/month        │
│  ─────────────────────────────          │
│  FRS S$220,400 → ~S$1,600/month        │
│  ─────────────────────────────          │
│  BRS S$110,200 → ~S$820/month          │
│                                          │
│  Which one will YOU be on at 55?       │
│                                          │
│  Check your CPF 55 Readiness Report →  │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Colour Palette:**
- Background: Light grey (#F5F5F5) or white
- ERS tier: Gold (#F4A847)
- FRS tier: Navy (#003366)
- BRS tier: Steel grey (#9CA3AF)
- Question text: Dark navy, bold

**Canva Build Instructions:**
1. Three horizontal bands stacked, ERS at top (smallest but most prominent with gold)
2. Payout callout for each band
3. Bold question below
4. CTA button at bottom

## Creative Concept 4 — Variant D (Personal Photo + Stat)
**Emotional Direction:** Relatability + Urgency + Human connection

**Image Description:**
Lifestyle photo of a Singaporean professional in their early–mid 50s (not an obvious stock photo). Looking thoughtful or engaged. Stat overlay adds data credibility to the human-forward visual.

**Stat overlay text:**
> "30% of CPF members turning 55 in 2022 didn't meet the FRS.
> Does your retirement plan cover the gap?"

**AI Image Prompt:**
> "A Singaporean man in his early 50s, in business casual attire, sitting at a coffee table with a cup of coffee, looking thoughtfully at his phone or laptop. Modern Singapore apartment or coffee shop background, slightly blurred. Natural light. Authentic, not stock-photo stiff. --ar 4:5"

**Overlay technique:**
- Semi-transparent dark gradient covers bottom 40% of image
- White text over gradient
- Source citation in small grey text: "(Source: CPF Board Parliamentary Reply 2022)"

## Compliance Checklist for All Angle 2 Creatives

- [ ] CPF payout figures labelled as "approximate" or "based on published CPF Board data"
- [ ] No use of official CPF Board logo or government branding (use generic financial icons)
- [ ] Disclaimer text included: "Information is general in nature and not personalised financial advice. Refer to CPF Board for personalised CPF projections."
- [ ] Your MAS licence number visible somewhere in the ad (footer or linked landing page)
- [ ] 2026 figures used where applicable (ERS expanded, salary ceiling update)
- [ ] Source citations shown for all statistics used
