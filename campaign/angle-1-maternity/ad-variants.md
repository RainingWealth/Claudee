# Angle 1 — Maternity → Education Gap: Ad Variants

**Campaign Goal:** Acquire leads at ~S$2 CPL. Use life-event trigger (pregnancy/new baby) to enter funnel, then bridge to education planning and investment conversation.

**Key Rule:** Do NOT change Variant A (the proven $2 CPL ad) without A/B testing first. Run Variant A as control, test B and C.

---

## Ad Variant A — Existing High-Performer (Keep As Is)
**Emotional Hook:** Practicality + Zero Friction + Life-event Urgency

**Primary Text:**
> Getting ready for your baby? Don't stress — grab this free Baby Starter Pack designed for new parents in Singapore.
>
> Inside:
> ✔ Hospital bag checklist
> ✔ Baby essentials list
> ✔ Delivery cost guide (real SG examples)
> ✔ First-month care cheat sheet
> ✔ Newborn savings planner

**Headline:** Expecting in 2026? Claim Your Free Baby Starter Pack

**Description:** Free baby planner + cost guide for new parents.

**CTA Button:** Download

**Creative Direction:** Warm, reassuring photo of prepared nursery or smiling pregnant couple. Soft pastel tones (blush pink, mint green). No financial imagery — this is a practical resource ad.

---

## Ad Variant B — Education Shock (Test)
**Emotional Hook:** Loss Aversion + Parental Identity + Concrete Gap Number

**Primary Text:**
> You're planning for baby's arrival — but are you planning for what comes AFTER?
>
> Singapore university fees have increased every single year since 2010. A single overseas degree by 2043 could cost your family S$300,000+.
>
> Most parents don't realise the gap until it's too late. Only 45% feel financially prepared.
>
> Download our free Baby Starter Pack — it now includes a Baby 0–18 Cost Timeline showing exactly what you'll need, year by year, so nothing catches you off guard.

**Headline:** University in 2043 Could Cost $300K+

**Description:** Free Baby Starter Pack + 18-year cost timeline for SG parents.

**CTA Button:** Download Free Guide

**Creative Direction:** Split-image or side-by-side. Left: happy newborn photo. Right: simple cost timeline bar chart showing costs climbing from Year 0 to Year 18. Large bold text: "S$300,000+" on right side. Clean white background for right panel.

**Note:** Test this as a secondary creative against Variant A. CPL may be slightly higher but lead quality (meeting conversion) should improve.

## Testing Recommendation

| Variant | Predicted CPL | Use Case |
|---------|------------|----------|
| A (existing) | ~S$2 | Control — always run |
| B (education shock) | S$3–8 | Test for meeting conversion |
| C (guilt reframe) | S$3–6 | Test for volume + quality balance |
| D (urgency) | S$2–5 | Seasonal test (pre-school registration period, etc.) |

Run A vs B first (2 weeks, equal budget split). If B generates more meetings per dollar spent even with higher CPL, shift budget. Keep A running regardless as volume engine.
