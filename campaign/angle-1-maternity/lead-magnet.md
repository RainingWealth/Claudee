# Angle 1 — Lead Magnet: Baby Starter Pack (Upgraded)

**Name:** The Singapore Baby Starter Pack — Hospital, Home & Your 18-Year Head Start
**Format:** PDF, 10–12 pages
**Build Tool:** Canva (use "Document" template, A4 size, export as PDF)
**Alternative Tools:** Gamma.app (faster, AI-assisted design), Notion to PDF

**Purpose:** Deliver immediate practical value (hospital/home) while planting the education funding seed. The last 2 pages bridge from "baby prep" to "18-year cost awareness" — setting up the WhatsApp follow-up conversation.

### Page 1 — Hospital Bag Checklist
**Content:**
- For Mum: Comfortable nightgown, nursing bra, toiletries, snacks, phone charger, insurance card, birth plan (if applicable)
- For Baby: Onesies x3, receiving blanket, hat, mittens, car seat (for discharge)
- For Partner: Change of clothes, snacks, laptop/iPad, hospital bag with overnight essentials
- Admin: IC, marriage cert, insurance cards, pre-registration confirmation

**Design Note:** Use a simple checkbox list format. Tick box icons on the left. Easy to print and use.

---

### Page 2 — Baby Essentials Shopping List
**Content (categorised):**
- Sleep: Cot/bassinet, mattress, sleep sack, monitor
- Feeding: Bottles, steriliser, formula (if not breastfeeding), nursing pillow
- Diapering: Nappies (NB + Size 1), wipes, changing mat, barrier cream
- Bathing: Baby bathtub, mild wash, soft towels
- Gear: Stroller, infant carrier, car seat
- Clothing: Onesies, scratch mittens, socks (0–3m range)

**Design Note:** Table format with "Nice to Have" vs "Must Have" columns. Add Singapore brand names where relevant (Bumble Bee, Pigeon, etc.).

---

### Page 3 — SG Delivery Cost Guide (Real Examples)
**Content:**
- Restructured from existing pack
- Table comparing: SGH, KKH, Mount Alvernia, Thomson Medical, Mount Elizabeth
- Columns: Normal delivery (subsidised), Normal delivery (private), C-section (subsidised), C-section (private)
- Estimated range: S$3,000–S$18,000+ depending on hospital and ward class
- Medisave claimable amount: Up to S$900 (normal) / S$2,150 (C-section)
- MediShield Life + Integrated Shield Plan coverage notes

**Design Note:** Simple table with hospital logos (can use text names). Bold the "After Medisave" final cost column. Add note: "Prices indicative as of 2025 — confirm with hospital."

---

### Page 4 — First Month Care Cheat Sheet
**Content:**
- Week 1: Newborn checks, hearing test, heel prick test, jaundice monitoring
- Feeding schedule: Newborn feeds every 2–3 hours (8–12 times/day)
- Sleep: 16–18 hours/day in 2–4 hour stretches (normal)
- Umbilical cord care: Keep dry, falls off in 1–3 weeks
- Baby blues vs postnatal depression: Know the difference, seek help
- First paed appointment: Usually within 3–5 days of discharge
- Government support: Baby Bonus Scheme (S$3,000 first account, S$3,000 Government co-matching), MediSave Grant for Newborns (S$4,000)

**Design Note:** Timeline or week-by-week layout. Add government scheme amounts prominently — these resonate with new parents.

---

### Page 5 — First Year Budget Snapshot
**Content:**
- Monthly infant care centre: S$600–S$2,000 (after subsidy)
- Domestic helper (if applicable): ~S$800–1,200/month
- Estimated year 1 total baby expenses: S$15,000–S$30,000
- Most surprising costs: Medical visits, enrichment classes, photography
- Tip: Baby Bonus CDA (Child Development Account) — use it for childcare fees, medical, education

**Design Note:** Visual pie chart or bar chart showing breakdown. Use real numbers. This builds trust.

### Page 6 — YOUR BABY'S 18-YEAR COST TIMELINE (THE BRIDGE PAGE)
**This is the new content — the education gap seed.**

**Headline:** From First Nappy to First Day of University — What It Really Costs

**Content table:**

| Life Stage | Approximate Annual Cost (SG$) |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| Age 0–3: Infant care + baby needs | S$20,000–30,000/year |
| Age 4–6: Preschool + enrichment | S$10,000–18,000/year |
| Age 7–12: Primary + tuition | S$6,000–15,000/year |
| Age 13–17: Secondary + tuition | S$8,000–18,000/year |
| Age 18–22: University (local) | S$15,000–20,000/year |
| Age 18–22: University (overseas) | S$60,000–90,000/year |

**Pull stat:** "Singapore families now spend S$1.8 BILLION on private tuition annually (2023). The average household spends S$105 per month on tuition alone." — Straits Times / HES 2023

**Key message:** "University fees have increased every single year since 2010. A local degree for your baby born today could cost S$80,000–120,000 by 2043. An overseas degree: S$250,000–400,000."

**Design Note:** This should feel informational, not alarmist. Use a calm blue/green colour palette. The goal is to plant the question: "Am I on track?" NOT to sell anything yet.

### Page 7 — The Simple Funding Formula
**This is the action bridge — creates the "I need a plan" moment.**

**Headline:** How Much Do You Need to Set Aside Each Month?

**Content — 3 scenarios:**

| If you start now (baby aged 0–1) | Monthly amount | Projected fund at 18 |
|-----------------------------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Conservative (savings account, ~1.5% p.a.) | S$800/month | ~S$175,000 |
| Moderate (investment mix, ~5% p.a.) | S$500/month | ~S$175,000 |
| Growth (equity-heavy, ~7% p.a.) | S$350/month | ~S$175,000 |

*Projections are illustrative only and not a guarantee of returns. Actual results will vary.*

**Key message:** "The earlier you start, the less you need to set aside every month. Waiting 5 years to start could mean needing to save S$300 more per month to reach the same goal."

**CTA:** "Want a projection customised to your child's age and your target (local vs overseas)? Message me on WhatsApp — takes 10 minutes."

**Design Note:** Simple 3-row table is clean and scannable. The disclaimer is important (MAS compliance). The CTA should include your WhatsApp number as a clickable link.
