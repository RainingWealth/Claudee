# Angle 1 — Follow-Up Scripts: Maternity/Education Gap

**Golden Rule:** You are not selling. You are delivering a resource and offering help. The meeting must feel like a service, not a sales call.

**Response Time Target:**
- Priority leads (income S$8k+, savings S$300+): Within 5 minutes
- Standard leads: Within 2 hours
- Nurture leads: Within 24 hours (automated email is fine)

---

## Step 1 — WhatsApp Opener (Send within 5 minutes of lead)

### Template A — Warm, Practical (Recommended for most leads)

> Hi [First Name]! I saw you downloaded the Baby Starter Pack — congrats on the new arrival (or exciting news coming soon)!
>
> Your free pack includes the hospital checklist, delivery cost guide, and the new 18-year cost timeline.
>
> Quick question — would you find it helpful if I put together a personalised projection showing what your child's education might cost by the time they're 18, and what you'd need to set aside each month? Takes about 15 minutes over a quick call, completely free.
>
> No pressure at all — just a resource if it's useful to you!

*(Do NOT say "financial planning", "investment", "insurance" in this first message.)*

---

### Template B — More Direct (For leads who answered "Yes, I've been meaning to start")

> Hi [First Name]! Thanks for grabbing the Baby Starter Pack!
>
> I noticed you mentioned you've been thinking about starting a plan for your little one — that's exactly what I help new parents with here in Singapore.
>
> I can put together a quick personalised projection for you — basically, here's what university will likely cost when your child is 18, and here's the simplest way to get there. Takes 15 minutes, free, no obligation.
>
> Would that be useful? What time works for a quick Zoom call?

---

### Template C — Nurture (For lower-income or "just downloading" leads)

> Hi [First Name], thanks for downloading the Baby Starter Pack! Hope the hospital checklist and delivery cost guide are helpful.
>
> I've also added a new section on the 18-year cost timeline — give it a read when things settle down.
>
> If you ever have questions about planning for your child's future in Singapore, feel free to message me. Happy to help!

*(No meeting push. Let them come to you.)*

---

## Step 2 — Soft Qualification (If they reply positively)

When they say "yes, that sounds helpful" or "sure, tell me more":

> Great! Just so I can make the projection relevant to you — a few quick questions:
>
> 1. How old is your baby (or when are you due)?
> 2. Are you thinking local university, or keeping overseas as an option?
> 3. Roughly how much would you be comfortable setting aside monthly — even if it's a small amount to start?
>
> No right or wrong answers — just helps me tailor the numbers to your situation.

---

## Step 3 — Meeting Booking Script

Once qualified, send calendar link:

> Perfect, thanks for sharing that! Based on what you've told me, I can put together a projection that shows:
>
> ✔ Estimated total cost (local vs overseas) for your child by 2043
> ✔ Monthly savings needed starting now vs waiting 3 years
> ✔ 2–3 simple approaches used by Singapore families in your situation
>
> Here's a link to book a 20-minute Zoom call at a time that suits you: [Calendly link]
>
> It's free and there's no commitment — I'll just share the numbers and you decide if it's useful.

---

## Step 4 — Meeting Framing (What to say in the first 2 minutes of the call)

> "Thanks for jumping on — I know you've got a lot on your plate with a new baby. This won't feel like a sales call. What I've done is pull together an education cost projection based on what you told me, and I'll walk you through 2–3 ways other families in Singapore are handling this. At the end, if it makes sense to take it further, we can talk. If not, no problem — the projection is yours to keep."

**Tone goal:** Calm, practical, like a knowledgeable friend.

---

## Objection Handling

### "I'll think about it / I'll do it later"
> "Totally understand — there's a lot happening right now. Just so you know, the main reason families start early is that waiting 3 years means needing roughly S$200–300 more per month to reach the same goal. I can send you a 1-page summary by WhatsApp so you have the numbers when you're ready to decide. Would that help?"

### "I already have a plan / I already have an FA"
> "That's great — it means you're already thinking about this. I'm not here to replace what you have. If anything, it might be worth a second pair of eyes to make sure the education component is specifically covered. Most plans I review don't separate education funding from general savings — and they end up short. But if you're comfortable with what you have, that's completely fine too."

### "I'm not ready to invest / we don't have much money right now"
> "No pressure at all. Actually, the earlier you know the numbers, the better — even if you start with S$100–200 a month, it compounds. The projection I'll share is just about awareness. Nothing to commit to. Want me to send it over so you have it as a reference?"

### "Is this insurance or investment?"
> "Neither, actually — at least not at this stage. Right now I'm just sharing the cost projection and funding options available. Whether it involves insurance, investment, CPF top-ups, or a mix depends entirely on your situation. I'll explain all the options and you choose what fits. No pressure to do anything."
