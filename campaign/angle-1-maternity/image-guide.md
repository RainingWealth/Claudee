# Angle 1 — Image Guide: Maternity/Education Gap

**Platform specs:**
- Feed: 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait — takes more screen space, recommended)
- Stories/Reels: 1080x1920px
- Link ad (if using website): 1200x628px
- All images: RGB colour, JPG/PNG, max 20% text overlay (keep text minimal in image)

---

## Creative Concept 1 — Variant A (Existing Ad)
**Emotional Direction:** Warmth + Practicality + Zero Anxiety

**Image Description:**
A warm, natural-light photo of a prepared nursery OR a smiling couple (or mother alone) holding a small pile of baby items (onesies, baby shoes, blanket). Soft, uncluttered setting. Real-feeling, not stock-photo stiff.

**Recommended Stock Photo Search Terms:**
- Shutterstock/Unsplash: "Singapore parents nursery", "pregnant couple home singapore", "baby shower preparation Asia"
- iStock: "Asian pregnant woman relaxed home"

**AI Image Prompt (Adobe Firefly / DALL-E 3 / Midjourney):**
> "A warm, natural light photo of a young Asian couple sitting on the floor of a tidy nursery in Singapore, surrounded by neatly folded baby clothes and a checklist. They are smiling and relaxed. Soft beige and mint green tones. Lifestyle photography style, no text."

**Text Overlay (keep minimal):**
- None preferred — let the headline in the ad do the work
- Optional: Small badge in corner: "FREE DOWNLOAD" in white on mint green circle

**Colour Palette:**
- Background: Soft white (#FFFFFF) or warm beige (#FAF7F0)
- Accent: Mint green (#B5EAD7), Blush pink (#F7CAC9)
- Text (if any): Deep navy (#1C3557) for high contrast

**Canva Template Search:**
- Search "baby checklist" or "pregnancy guide" in Canva templates
- Remove all existing text, replace with your minimal overlay
- Export as PNG at 1080x1350px

## Creative Concept 2 — Variant B (Education Shock)
**Emotional Direction:** Wake-up call + Authority + Data credibility

**Image Description:**
Split-frame or layered design. Left half: warm photo of a newborn or parent holding baby. Right half: clean white panel with bold black stat text. Simple, editorial feel.

**Right Panel Text Layout:**
```
University in 2043?

S$300,000+
(for one overseas degree)

Only 45% of SG parents
feel prepared.
```

**Text Styling:**
- "S$300,000+" in large bold serif font (e.g., Canva's "IM Fell English" or "Playfair Display")
- Font size: 72–96pt for the dollar amount
- Supporting text: 24–32pt, regular weight
- Colour: Deep navy text on white background

**AI Image Prompt (left half photo):**
> "Close-up lifestyle photo of an Asian newborn baby sleeping peacefully wrapped in a soft white blanket, warm natural light, blurred soft background, photorealistic, no text"

**Canva Build Instructions:**
1. Create 1080x1080px canvas
2. Split 50/50 with a dividing line
3. Left: Insert baby photo
4. Right: White rectangle, add text as above
5. Source citation in small text at bottom: "(Source: University fee projections, insurer calculators)"

**Colour Palette:**
- Photo side: Natural warm tones
- Data side: White (#FFFFFF) background, Navy (#1C3557) text, Gold (#F4A847) accent for dollar amount

## Creative Concept 3 — Variant C (Guilt Reframe)
**Emotional Direction:** Social proof + Relatable + Non-threatening

**Image Description:**
Lifestyle photo of a parent (mother OR father — test both) at a kitchen table or home office, looking at laptop or phone, with a baby nearby (in bouncer, playmat, etc.). Everyday Singapore home feel. Normal, relatable.

**Text Overlay:**
```
S$1.8 BILLION
spent on tuition in Singapore last year.

Is YOUR child's
university fund keeping up?

[DOWNLOAD FREE GUIDE]
```

**Design notes:**
- Use a semi-transparent dark overlay on the bottom third of the photo for text readability
- Stat ("S$1.8 BILLION") in large, bold white text
- Source citation: "(Straits Times / Household Expenditure Survey 2023)"
- Button-style CTA at the bottom: White text on navy rectangle

**AI Image Prompt:**
> "Candid lifestyle photo of a young Asian mother working on her laptop at a kitchen table, her 6-month-old baby playing in a bouncer nearby. Natural light from window, modern Singapore HDB home interior, warm tones, photorealistic style"

**Canva Build Instructions:**
1. 1080x1350px canvas (portrait for feed)
2. Full-bleed photo (top 70%)
3. Dark gradient overlay (bottom 40%): Black at 60% opacity
4. White text layered on gradient
5. CTA rectangle: Navy with white text at bottom

## Creative Concept 4 — Variant D (Urgency/Emotional)
**Emotional Direction:** Pure emotion, minimal text, maximum scroll-stop

**Image Description:**
Extreme close-up of newborn baby feet or hands — universally emotional image. Almost no text overlay. The emotion does the work.

**Minimal Text Overlay:**

## A/B Testing Creative Notes

- Test square (1080x1080) vs portrait (1080x1350) — portrait typically gets more feed real estate and higher CTR
- Test photo of baby vs photo of parent vs data-heavy text graphic — data graphic often performs surprisingly well in financial contexts
- Test warm tones (blush/mint) vs clinical white/navy — warm tones work better for maternity angle; clinical works better for data-shock variants
- After 500 impressions per creative, pause underperformers and double budget on winner
