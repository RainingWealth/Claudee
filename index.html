About Rayvern Chng

A Financial Partner Who Understands What's at Stake

When you've built a business from the ground up, you understand that the right protection isn't a luxury—it's a necessity. I'm Rayvern Chng, a certified financial advisor based in Singapore, and I specialise in helping business owners and key corporate leaders build comprehensive financial strategies that protect what they've worked so hard to create.

With qualifications including AWP (CFP), IBFQ, AEPP, and recognition as an MDRT member, I bring both technical expertise and genuine care to every client relationship. Whether it's ensuring your business survives the unexpected loss of a key person, structuring your estate for seamless wealth transfer, or creating a lifelong financial plan for a loved one with special needs—I'm here to guide you through every decision with clarity and confidence.
