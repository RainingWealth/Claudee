Corporate Risk Management

Protect Your Enterprise Against the Unexpected

In Singapore, SMEs make up 99% of all enterprises — yet over 60% lack adequate key person coverage. What would happen to your business if you or a key partner could no longer work? I work with business owners, MDs, and HR leaders to design corporate insurance strategies that protect against personnel loss and maintain continuity.

What We Cover

Comprehensive Corporate Protection

Strategic insurance solutions designed for Singapore businesses — from startups to established enterprises.

Key Person Insurance

Protect against the financial impact of losing a founder, director, or critical employee. Covers revenue loss, recruitment costs, and debt obligations tied to key personnel.

Group Insurance & Employee Benefits

Attract and retain top talent with comprehensive group health, life, and disability coverage tailored to your team's needs and your budget.

Business Continuity Planning

Structure insurance and financial reserves to keep your business running during disruption. Ensure operations continue smoothly regardless of unexpected events.

Buy-Sell Agreement Funding

When a business partner exits, proper funding ensures a smooth transition of ownership without draining business capital. Protect shareholder value at every stage.
