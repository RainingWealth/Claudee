Special Needs Planning

Every Individual Is Unique. So Is Our Care Plan.

For parents and caregivers, one question weighs heaviest: "Who will take care of them when I'm gone?" In Singapore, 3–5% of the population lives with some form of disability — yet many families lack a structured financial plan for lifelong care. This requires specialised knowledge and deep empathy.

In Detail

Key Planning Instruments

SNTC Trust Planning

The Special Needs Trust Company (SNTC) is Singapore's only non-profit trust company dedicated to persons with special needs. We help families set up and fund SNTC trusts to provide long-term financial security, ensuring funds are managed responsibly and disbursed for your loved one's care.

CPF Special Needs Savings Scheme

The SNSS allows CPF members to nominate their savings to provide regular monthly payouts to a special needs beneficiary, rather than a lump sum that may be difficult to manage. We guide families through the application and optimisation process.

Insurance & Care Funding

Structure insurance policies to fund long-term care, therapy, and living expenses. Proceeds are channelled through appropriate trust structures to ensure responsible, ongoing financial support for your special needs loved one.

Letter of Intent & Care Plan

Document your loved one's routines, preferences, medical needs, and care instructions in detail. This ensures continuity of care regardless of changes in caregivers, giving future carers a complete picture of your loved one's world.
