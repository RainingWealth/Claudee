Services Offered

Comprehensive Estate Planning Solutions

From will writing to business succession, we provide a full suite of estate planning services tailored to your unique circumstances.

General

Will Writing & Review

Ensure your assets are distributed according to your wishes — not by a government formula. We draft, review, and update wills to reflect your current intentions.

Trust Structures

Living trusts, testamentary trusts, and insurance trusts — we help you establish the right trust structures to protect and distribute your wealth efficiently.

Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA)

Appoint trusted decision-makers to act on your behalf should you lose mental capacity. An LPA ensures your personal and financial affairs are managed by someone you trust.

Advanced Medical Directive (AMD)

Document your healthcare wishes clearly and legally. An AMD ensures your end-of-life preferences are respected and relieves your family of difficult decisions.

Advanced Care Planning (ACP)

Go beyond the AMD with comprehensive care instructions that cover medical treatments, care preferences, and personal values for future healthcare scenarios.

For Business Owners

Shareholder Agreement

Protect business ownership transitions with properly structured shareholder agreements that prevent disputes and ensure continuity when partners exit or pass on.

Business Trust & Succession

Ensure a smooth company handover to the next generation or designated successors. We structure business trusts and succession plans to preserve your enterprise's legacy.

Important Considerations

Details That Make the Difference

Small oversights in estate planning can create major problems for your loved ones. Here are critical factors many people overlook.

Schedule of Assets

Attach a comprehensive asset schedule to your estate plan. Without it, your family may struggle to locate bank accounts, insurance policies, investments, and property — leading to unclaimed assets.

Storage of Will

Original wills stored in restricted safes or safety deposit boxes can create access difficulties for your family when they need it most. Consider accessible and secure storage solutions.

Guardianship

Designate appropriate guardians for your children. Without a clear appointment, the court decides who raises your children — and their choice may not align with yours.

Legal Age

Direct bequests to minors become inaccessible until they turn 18. Without proper trust structures, your children's inheritance may be locked away precisely when they need it most.
